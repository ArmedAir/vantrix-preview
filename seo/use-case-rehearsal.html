Using an AI Companion to Rehearse Hard Conversations

Salary negotiations, boundaries, apologies — the exact drill that turns dread into a script you've already survived once.

VVantrix Journal·Use case·Sep 2026·7 min read

On this page

The methodThree drillsWhy it works

The method

  1. Pick the right archetype. Directness high, warmth medium: Victoria or Mason. You want a sparring partner, not a cheerleader.
  2. State the stakes. Tell them the real conversation, the real person, the real fear. Memory means next session they'll ask how it went — that accountability loop is the point.
  3. Run it three ways. Best case, worst case, and weird case (they cry, they get angry, they agree too fast).
  4. Debrief the flinch. The most valuable question in the whole exercise: "Where did I get smaller?" The answer is usually minute two.

Three drills

The raise

Mason: "Name your number. Now defend it without apologizing." Then he plays the CFO and gives you nothing. By round three the number stops sounding foreign in your mouth.

The boundary

Victoria doesn't let you over-explain. "One sentence. The justification weakens it." You will try to add a second sentence. She will notice.

The apology

Charlotte insists you apologize for the specific thing — "not the weather around it." Precision is what separates repair from performance.

Why it works

Anxiety rehearses the conversation badly — always the catastrophic version. A companion rehearses it accurately, infinitely, and then remembers the real one afterward. The script is half the value; the debrief is the other half.
