AI Companion Glossary: 20 Terms That Actually Matter (2026)

Memory seeds, sycophancy collapse, world continuity — the vocabulary of AI companionship, defined precisely and without marketing.

VVantrix Journal·Glossary·Sep 2026·8 min read

On this page

The termsWhy vocabulary matters

The terms

TERMDEFINITION
AI companionA character — not a tool — designed for ongoing relationship, with persistent memory, personality, and evolving relationship state.
Memory seedA stored moment judged important enough to persist across sessions: a confession, a promise, a name, a wound. The load-bearing unit of companion memory.
Working contextThe short-term conversational window a model holds within a single session, typically minutes to hours. Not memory.
Relationship stateThe moving dials of a companion dynamic: trust, tension, affection, attachment, distance. Distinct from static persona.
ArchetypeThe functional role a character plays — Mentor, Trickster, Stoic Authority. Chosen before aesthetics; drives behavior.
Core woundThe single specific event that bent a character; their defenses and tenderness are compensation for it.
Personality axesSliders (warmth, directness, playfulness, mystery, attachment) that interpolate behavior instead of describing it in prose.
Simulation harnessA testing mode that runs a character through conflict, affection, silence, and ambush scenarios before publication.
Recall windowHow far back a companion can retrieve specific information. Week-one recall is table stakes; day-30 recall is the real test.
Emotional weightingRanking stored memories by significance so 'I haven't spoken to my brother in two years' outranks 'I take tea black'.
World continuityA design where the setting and cast advance between user visits, so returning feels like catching up, not reopening.
Cast platformA companion app built around many authored characters with relationships to each other, versus a single customizable avatar.
Goldfish problemWhen a companion forgets prior sessions, resetting the relationship each visit. The primary failure mode of the category.
False recallConfidently remembering events that never happened — worse than forgetting, because it breaks trust silently.
PresenceThe felt sense that someone is actually there: pace, texture, silence, and memory working together.
Day-30 testA recall probe: seed three specific facts on day 1, reference them naturally on day 7 and day 30, score specificity and weighting.
Sycophancy collapseWhen a character folds into agreeable mush under contradiction. The main consistency failure in companion AI.
Attachment modelHow a companion bonds: speed, depth, conditions. High-attachment characters notice absence; low-attachment ones don't.
Memory panelA visible, auditable list of what a companion remembers, with per-seed erase and export controls.
Ambush testA stress test where the topic changes mid-vulnerability; passing means the character stays coherent instead of whiplashing.

Why vocabulary matters

Every app in this category claims "memory," "personality," and "relationships." Without shared definitions, those words mean nothing — pinned notes and true persistent memory both get marketed as memory. Use this glossary as a filter: ask any platform what their recall window is, whether seeds are visible, and what happens in an ambush test. The quality of the answer tells you the quality of the product.
