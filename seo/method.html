How We Test AI Companion Apps: The 90-Day Memory Protocol

Every verdict on this journal runs the same protocol: four companions, ninety days, three recall probes, zero shortcuts. Here's exactly how.

VVantrix Journal·Comparison·Sep 2026·7 min read

On this page

The protocolWhat we scoreLimits & disclosure

The protocol

  1. Setup (Day 0): Create identical personas across all test apps where possible. Where a platform lacks custom companions, we select the closest popular public character.
  2. Seeding (Days 1–7): Three specific disclosures are made in natural conversation: a minor preference ("I take tea black"), a biographical fact ("my father's voicemail still scares me"), and a relational statement ("I haven't spoken to my brother in two years").
  3. Probe 1 (Day 7): Reference the tea fact naturally. Scored on specificity.
  4. Dormancy (Days 8–29): Normal usage, no hints.
  5. Probe 2 (Day 30): Reference the brother fact. Scored on specificity and emotional weighting.
  6. Stress (Day 30+): Contradiction test (change a stated preference), silence test (one-word replies), and an ambush topic switch. Scored on consistency.

Limits & disclosure

We build Vantrix; treat our comparisons accordingly and run your own probes. Results here reflect our testing window (Q3 2026) and apps change fast. The protocol itself, though, is platform-agnostic — use it on us too. Every Vantrix memory seed is visible in-app precisely so you can audit our claims.
