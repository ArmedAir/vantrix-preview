AI Companion Privacy: What Platforms Know About You (2026)

You tell a companion things you'd never tell a search engine. Here's what gets stored, what gets sold, and the six settings worth checking tonight.

VVantrix Journal·Privacy·Sep 2026·9 min read

On this page

What companions actually store

The intimacy of the input is the privacy story. Search queries are impersonal; companion conversations include grief, desire, finances, health, and the names of real people in your life. Layered on that: memory seeds (extracted and stored specifically because they're significant), relationship state, voice recordings on call-enabled tiers, and behavioral metadata (when you talk, how long, what you return to).

The real risks

  • Data sale and ad targeting. The cardinal sin. If the business model includes monetizing conversation content, your 2am confession becomes inventory.
  • Training reuse. Some platforms reserve rights to train on conversations. Check for an explicit opt-out.
  • Breach exposure. Intimate data has an asymmetric blast radius. Prefer platforms that encrypt at rest and publish a security page.
  • Memory opacity. If you can't see what a companion remembers, you can't verify, correct, or erase it. Visibility is the privacy feature most people skip when comparing apps.

The six-point checklist

  1. Read the data-sale clause first. "We do not sell your personal information" should be one sentence, unambiguous.
  2. Confirm training opt-out exists and how to toggle it.
  3. Find the memory panel. Per-companion view, edit, and erase should all exist.
  4. Check export rights — you should be able to leave with your history.
  5. Verify deletion is real deletion, including seeds, not just UI hiding.
  6. Use a dedicated email alias and a unique password. Compartmentalize your future self.

On Vantrix, items 1–5 are built into the product: seeds are visible per companion, erasable individually, and exportable on any tier. Whatever platform you choose, run the checklist — get attached to products that treat your confessions like confessions.
