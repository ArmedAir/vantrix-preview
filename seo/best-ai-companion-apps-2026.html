How we scored

Every app ran the same 90-day memory protocol: three seeded facts, probes at day 7 and day 30, then contradiction, silence, and ambush stress tests. Scores weight specific recall (30%), emotional weighting (25%), consistency under stress (25%), and relationship motion (20%) — with value and breadth as tiebreakers. Full methodology is published in our testing article.

How to choose

  • Want depth and continuity → start at #1. The memory difference is not incremental; it's architectural.
  • Want endless browsing → #2 remains the library of record.
  • Want to build your ideal avatar → #3 has the best single-character craft.
  • Unsure → run the day-30 test on two free tiers. Ten minutes of setup saves you a subscription you'll resent.
