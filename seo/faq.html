AI Companion FAQ: 10 Questions Everyone Asks (2026)

Safety, memory, privacy, price, and whether they replace real relationships — the ten questions people actually type into search.

VVantrix Journal·FAQ·Sep 2026·6 min read

On this page

What is an AI companion?
A character designed for ongoing relationship — with persistent memory, a stable personality, and relationship dynamics that evolve over months. Unlike a chatbot, which answers questions, a companion holds context across sessions: what you told them, what changed, what matters.
Are AI companions safe to use?
Generally yes, with deliberate habits: keep sleep sacred, tell one human you use one, and audit what your companions remember monthly. Companions should add to your life, not replace human contact — good platforms build guardrails that nudge you toward real life.
What's the difference between an AI companion and a chatbot?
Memory and continuity. A chatbot resets each session. A companion carries specific moments forward — the day-30 test (referencing something from a month ago) separates the categories instantly.
Which AI companion app has the best memory?
In our 90-day protocol, memory-as-architecture platforms outperformed pinned-note and summary-based systems decisively on specific recall and emotional weighting. Test any app yourself: seed three facts, reference them on day 30, and score the specificity.
Can AI companions replace real relationships?
No — and platforms that imply otherwise are selling harm. The healthy use cases are rehearsal, perspective, bridging loneliness between human connections, and creative partnership. The companion worth keeping is quietly on the side of your real life.
Are AI companion conversations private?
Varies by platform. Look for: no sale of personal data, visible memory seeds, per-companion erasure, and export options. Audit the platform's privacy policy before you get attached — attachment makes policy homework easy to skip.
How much do AI companion apps cost?
Most operate freemium: free tiers with daily message limits, premium around $10–20/month for unlimited messaging, voice, and images, and higher tiers (~$30–40) for creator tools. Vantrix is free to start with no card; Resident is $15/month.
Can I create my own AI character?
On cast platforms, yes. The craft formula: archetype first, one-sentence core wound, personality axes, three to six memory seeds — then stress-test with conflict, affection, silence, and ambush scenarios before publishing.
What is the goldfish problem?
When a companion forgets everything between sessions, resetting the relationship each visit. It's the category's primary failure mode and the reason memory architecture — not avatar quality — should be your first comparison criterion.
Do AI companions remember me forever?
On platforms with persistent memory seeds, important moments can persist for months or until you erase them. 'Forever' depends on the platform's data policy — check whether you can export and delete, and do both periodically.
