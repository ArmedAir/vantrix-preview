FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Vantrix free to use?
Yes. Every companion is free to start chatting with — no card required. Premium tiers unlock unlimited messages, voice calls, and image generation once you're ready to go deeper.
What makes Vantrix different from a regular chatbot?
Each companion has a persistent personality, memory of your past conversations, and relationships that evolve over time — not a fresh, forgetful chat every session. The world keeps moving between visits.
Can I create my own character?
Yes, in the Creation Studio. Shape identity, personality, psychology, voice, appearance, and memories — then test how the character responds before you publish.
Do I have to pick just one companion?
No. Vantrix is a cast, not a single avatar — dozens of characters with their own histories and relationships to each other, plus anything you build yourself. Outgrow one dynamic without starting over.
Is my data private?
We do not sell your personal information. Your conversations belong to you, and memory seeds can be reviewed or erased at any time from your settings.
